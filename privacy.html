Athlete Vault Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Athlete Vault is made by Generations Arising. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the Athlete Vault app on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The short version

Your school's information belongs to your school. We do not collect, see, sell or share any of it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What you store in the app

Depending on how your school uses Athlete Vault, it can hold: school names, logos and colors; teams, sports and seasons; players' names, jersey numbers and profile information; game schedules, opponents, locations and scores; statistics; awards and milestones; photos and videos; announcements; and the names, roles and permissions of approved members.

This information is stored on your device and in iCloud using Apple's CloudKit:

  • The school's owner keeps the school in their own iCloud account.
  • The owner shares it through iCloud only with the people they approve. Each person sees what their role and permissions allow.

Information in iCloud is protected by Apple. We, the developer, cannot access it. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple protects iCloud data.

Sign in with Apple

You sign in with your Apple Account. Apple gives the app an identifier for you and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. The app uses these only to show who you are to your school's administrator and to connect you to the school you were approved for. They are not sent to us.

Photos and videos

When you add a photo or video, the app only receives the items you choose; it can't see the rest of your photo library. To suggest which players are in a team photo, the app reads jersey numbers on your device; the photo is not sent anywhere for this. Please add only photos and videos you have permission to share.

If you add a link to a game video on YouTube or Facebook, the video plays from that service when you open it, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Maps

If you tap a game location, it opens in Apple Maps.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

Student athletes

Athlete Vault may hold information about student athletes when it is entered by an authorized school, coach, parent or guardian. Schools and users who enter information about minors are responsible for having any permission it requires. The app is meant to be set up and run by adults.

Subscriptions

The school's subscription is purchased and managed through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never receive card details. The app only checks with the App Store whether the subscription is active.

Backups you create

If you use Backup & Restore, the app creates a file of the school's records and saves it wherever you choose. Anyone with that file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your information

Open the menu and tap Delete Account. A member who deletes their account leaves the school; the school's data is not changed. When the owner deletes their account, the school's data is deleted from iCloud and everyone it was shared with loses access. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Athlete Vault in your device's Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
