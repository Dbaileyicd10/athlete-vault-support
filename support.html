Athlete Vault Support

Keep your school's teams, players, games, stats, awards, milestones, photos, videos and announcements in one place, shared with the people your school approves.

Contact us

Email generationsarising@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. Please include what happened, your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and the app version if you know it. Never send passwords by email.

Common questions

Who pays for Athlete Vault?
Only the school's owner (usually the athletic director or the person who sets up the school) needs a subscription. Principals, admins, coaches, parents and players who are approved for the school never pay.

I was invited to a school, but the app is asking me to subscribe.
Don't subscribe. Open the school access link your administrator sent you. It opens Athlete Vault so you can request access, or opens your school if you've already been approved. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account your administrator approved.

How do I invite coaches, parents and players?
The owner opens Share School Access and sends the school access link. Each person opens the link, signs in with Apple and taps Request Access. Then the owner opens Access Management, chooses each person's role (Admin, Principal, Coach, Parent or Player) and the teams or players they can see, and approves them.

I was approved but still can't see the school.
Close Athlete Vault completely and open it again, on Wi-Fi if you can. If it still says you don't have access, tap Copy Details on that screen and email the text to us.

How do I keep stats during a game?
Open Stat Tracker, choose the game and your five players on the floor, and tap a player each time they score, miss, rebound or make another play. The app remembers the game, quarter and lineup if you leave and come back. Owners, admins and coaches can use the Stat Tracker.

Who can post announcements?
The owner, admins and coaches. Open Announcements, pick the type of message and the sport, and edit the suggested wording before posting.

Who can delete records?
Only the school's owner can delete school records, so one mistaken tap can't erase a season. Admins and coaches can also edit and delete announcements.

Changes from another device aren't showing.
Give iCloud a minute to catch up, then close and reopen the app. Sync Status in the menu shows whether this device is up to date.

How do I back up my school?
Open Backup & Restore in the menu to save a backup file of the whole school.

How do I cancel the subscription?
On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Athlete Vault. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?
Open the menu and tap Delete Account. If you're a member, you leave the school and the school's data isn't changed. If you're the owner, your school's data is also deleted from iCloud and everyone you shared it with loses access. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings as described above.

Is our information private?
Yes. Your school's information is stored in iCloud and shared only with the people your school approves. We can't see it. Read the privacy policy.
